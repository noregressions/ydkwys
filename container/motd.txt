=====================================================================
  You Don't Know What You're Shipping — workshop container
=====================================================================
  Everything is preinstalled and prebuilt. Start here:

    ./scripts/tools-check.sh      confirm the toolchain (should be green)
    cat WORKSHOP.md               the route — or read the book PDF

  Docker commands here drive the HOST daemon (mounted socket), so
  S01/S02 images appear on your machine and their published ports are
  reachable from your own browser.

  S03/S04/S05 servers bind inside this container — use the curl
  commands from WORKSHOP.md in this shell, exactly as printed.
